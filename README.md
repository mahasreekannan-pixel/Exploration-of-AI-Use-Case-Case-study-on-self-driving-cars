# Exploration-of-AI-Use-Case-Case-study-on-self-driving-cars
# AIM

To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.

# THEORY

Self-driving cars, also known as autonomous vehicles, are vehicles that use Artificial Intelligence (AI), sensors, cameras, and advanced computing systems to move with reduced or no continuous human intervention, depending on their level of automation.

AI enables self-driving cars to understand road conditions, detect obstacles, recognize traffic signs, identify pedestrians, and make driving decisions. Machine learning and deep learning algorithms process the information collected by sensors and cameras to identify objects and predict their movements.

The major AI technologies used in self-driving cars are:
1. Computer Vision: Helps the vehicle identify road lanes, traffic lights, road signs, pedestrians, and other vehicles using camera images.
2. Machine Learning: Enables the vehicle to learn patterns from driving data and improve object recognition and prediction.
3. Sensor Fusion: Combines data from cameras, radar, LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging), and other sensors to build a more reliable understanding of the surroundings.
4. Decision-Making Algorithms: Help the vehicle decide when to stop, accelerate, turn, change lanes, or maintain a safe distance.
5. Path Planning and Navigation: Determines a suitable route and calculates a safe path to reach the destination.
6. Control Systems: Convert driving decisions into steering, braking, and acceleration commands.
Self-driving cars can improve transportation efficiency, support people with mobility difficulties, and help reduce accidents caused by human error. However, their performance depends on road conditions, sensor accuracy, weather, and the reliability of their AI systems. Human supervision and safety measures remain important for many autonomous driving systems.

# WORKING PRINCIPLE 

The working principle of an AI-based self-driving car is:

Sensors and Cameras → Data Collection → AI-Based Perception → Object Detection → Decision-Making → Path Planning → Vehicle Control → Safe Navigation
Working Steps
1. Data Collection: Cameras, radar, LiDAR, and other sensors collect information about the road, nearby vehicles, pedestrians, and obstacles.
2. AI-Based Perception: Computer vision and deep learning algorithms analyze the collected data to understand the vehicle's surroundings.
3. Object Detection: The AI system identifies road signs, traffic lights, lane markings, pedestrians, and other vehicles.
4. Localization: The vehicle determines its current position using positioning systems, sensor data, and digital maps.
5. Decision-Making: The AI system analyzes road conditions and decides whether to stop, accelerate, turn, or change lanes.
6. Path Planning: The system calculates a suitable and safe path to reach the destination while avoiding obstacles and following traffic rules.
7. Vehicle Control: The control system converts the planned actions into steering, braking, and acceleration commands.
8. Continuous Monitoring: Sensors continuously collect new data, allowing the AI system to update its decisions whenever road conditions change.
This process operates continuously, enabling the vehicle to respond to its surroundings in real time.
